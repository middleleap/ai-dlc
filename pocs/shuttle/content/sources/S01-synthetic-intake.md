# SHWF-001 synthetic intake — demo-v1

This is fictional demonstration content. It is not actual bank material, regulatory guidance or evidence of approval.

Fictional Bank Alpha is exploring a read-only account-aggregation proposition. A fictional third-party app would display linked account summaries. Stage A uses no real customer records, identifiers, balances, credentials, bank APIs or consent grants.

The demonstration asks whether a reviewer can see the scope, responsible roles, missing evidence and outstanding decisions from one case workspace. It does not seek launch permission.

Illustrative bank role: LFI / data sharing. The actual role, applicability, inherited controls, bank approval procedure and evidence requirements must be established with the real case owners later.

Initial open questions: what is in scope; who owns the operations work; what template may be shared; who has actual decision authority; and what technical/provider/source boundary is acceptable?
