## Governance Bodies

| Body | Abbrev. | Role |
|------|---------|------|
| Investment Screening Board | ISB | Stage 1 challenge and pre-screening (two gates: ISB1, ISB2) |
| Capital Investment Committee | CIC | Stage 2 funding approval |
| Central Finance | — | Reviews submissions, controls the CIC agenda and deadlines |
| Business Head | BH | Accountable business sponsor |

## Submission Sequence

```
[1] Business prepares Stage 1 Proposal (one-page pre-screening)
        ↓
[2] ISB1 review
        ↓
[3] ISB2 approval (must be secured before Central Finance submission)
        ↓
[4] Business prepares Detailed Business Case (all ISB actions completed)
        ↓
[5] Submit FINAL version to Central Finance (~2 weeks before CIC)
        ↓
[6] Central Finance review + Business Head sign-off (~1 week before CIC)
        ↓
[7] CIC meeting — business case presented for approval
```

**Critical rule:** The file submitted to Central Finance must be the FINAL version with ALL ISB
actions completed. Incomplete submissions are rejected as not meeting the CIC deadline.

## Typical CIC Cycle Timeline

CIC meetings occur on a regular cycle. Working backwards from the CIC date:

| When | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| CIC date minus ~3-4 weeks | ISB2 approval must be secured |
| CIC date minus ~2 weeks | Final business case submitted to Central Finance |
| CIC date minus ~1 week | Final approved version with Central Finance & Business Head sign-off |
| CIC date | Presentation to Capital Investment Committee |

## Stakeholder Sign-Offs

### Always mandatory:
1. **Investment Screening Board (ISB1 + ISB2)** — Challenge and pre-screen the proposal
2. **Business Head (BH)** — Business sponsor, accountable for the project
3. **Procurement** — Validate vendor selection, pricing, contract terms
4. **Finance** — Validate financial viability, NPV, cost assumptions
5. **Technology (IT)** — Validate technical feasibility, architecture impact, resource availability

### Conditional:
6. **Corporate Services** — Required for property and fit-out projects
7. **Compliance** — Required for regulatory projects

### Sign-off expectations
- Each stakeholder must provide: Yes/No decision + date + comments
- Sign-offs must be evidenced in the submission (not verbal)
- Central Finance will reject submissions without complete sign-off evidence

## Stage 1: ISB Pre-Screening

**Purpose:** Quick assessment of project viability before investing in a full business case.

**What the ISB evaluates:**
- Is the business rationale sound? Is there a simpler solution?
- Why now? Can it wait?
- Is the market assessment credible? Is this truly a differentiator?
- Are risks identified with credible mitigations?
- Are cost estimates realistic (T-shirt sizing)?
- Is the Build vs Buy decision justified?
- Can the project be broken into MVPs?
- Are timelines realistic given internal and external dependencies?

**Key deliverable:** One-page pre-screening summary covering project details, business assessment,
technical assessment, high-level plan, benefits overview, and solution selection rationale.

## Stage 2: CIC Detailed Business Case

**Purpose:** Comprehensive justification with detailed financials for CIC approval.

**What the CIC evaluates:**
- NPV positive at both 100% and 130% capital cost sensitivity
- IRR exceeds the hurdle rate (default 13% discount rate)
- KPIs are measurable with clear baselines and targets
- All costs accounted for including indirect tax (default 5%), contingency (default 10%), and
  shared IT infrastructure
- Risk mitigations are actionable, not generic
- Benefits are realistic and well-supported by assumptions
- Project organization is adequate

**Key deliverables:** Detailed Business Case PPTX + Project Costs Excel (NPV/IRR model)

## Project Categories

| Category | When to use | Approval emphasis |
|----------|------------|-------------------|
| Regulatory & Risk | External mandate (regulator, statutory deadline) | Timeline to deadline, penalty for non-compliance |
| KSOR (Keep the Show on Road) | Maintaining current operations, end-of-life replacement | Risk of not doing, operational continuity |
| Strategic | New capability, market expansion, competitive advantage | Market opportunity, differentiation, revenue potential |
| Efficiency | Cost reduction, process automation, FTE optimization | ROI, payback period, measurable savings |

## Common Rejection Reasons

- Incomplete sign-offs (most common)
- KPIs without baseline or target values
- Vague benefits ("improved efficiency" without currency figures or metrics)
- Missing 130% NPV sensitivity analysis
- Shared IT infrastructure costs not separated
- Indirect tax not applied to all cost lines
- No contingency on capital costs
- "Impact of not doing this project" section left blank
- Generic risks without specific mitigation actions
