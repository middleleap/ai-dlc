# Regulatory anchors — what each field is checked against

References are to the CBUAE Rulebook as published. Article numbering changes; the agent cites the reference and marks it "to be confirmed by Compliance" if it cannot verify the current text.

## The obligation itself

- **Consumer Protection Standards, Article 3 (Institutional Oversight).** Licensed Financial Institutions must apply the Consumer Protection Regulation and Standards to any new product or service, activity, action, change or event (3.1.2.2); must apply strict product and/or service approval processes before a new or updated financial product or service is released to the market (3.1.2.1(i)); and must run a Board-approved product approval framework that covers new products and subsequent amendments, with documented sign-off by authorised staff, Senior Management and control functions (3.2.1.13–3.2.1.17). This is why the NPA exists.
- **Consumer Protection Regulation (C 8/2020, in force 25 Dec 2020)** — the principles the Standards implement; conduct, disclosure, suitability, complaints.
- **Risk Management Regulation and Standards (C 153/2018)** — approval authority for introducing new products and material modifications (Art. 7); risk appetite, the three lines, and Islamic banking provisions (Art. 10).
- **Corporate Governance Regulation for Banks (C 83/2019) and Standards** — committee structures and Board accountability the sign-off roster reflects.

## Open Finance specifics

When the product uses Open Finance in either role, Section 5.1 must address the following — most of it set by the Standards and Nebras documents rather than the Regulation (see the `open-finance-uae` skill's references): the pricing model set by the Central Bank and reviewed with the Nebras Board; the notice period and dual running required for breaking changes; continuing conformance with the Standards version in force and its errata; FAPI 2.0, mTLS and SCA; consent lifecycle and expiry rules; certification paths (OIDF, Functional, CX, Live Proving); and liability allocation between LFI, TPP and Nebras.
