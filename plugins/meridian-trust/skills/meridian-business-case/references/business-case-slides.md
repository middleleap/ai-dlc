## Slide 2: Project Overview (Section 1)

Single-page summary containing ALL of:

**Left column:**
- **Project Name** and Ownership
- **Background and Business Demand** — context, requirements, business demands
- **Benefits (Financial & Non-Financial)** — cost savings, FTE saves, etc.
- **Project Scope** — deliverables, goals, tasks
- **Project Stakeholders** — list key stakeholders
- **Impact on Major System Clusters** — RAG status table:

| System | RAG | System | RAG |
|--------|-----|--------|-----|
| Core Banking | H/M/L | Origination & Onboarding | H/M/L |
| Payments Hub | H/M/L | Digital Channels | H/M/L/N/A |
| Cash Management | H/M/L | | |

RAG Legend: H = High, M = Medium, L = Low

> Replace this system list with the institution's own critical platform clusters when retargeting.

**Right column — Financial Summary Box:**
- Best Estimate Total Cost: CCY X.XX M
- Incremental Opex: CCY X.XX M
- Capex: CCY X.XX M
- Benefits Type: Financial / Non-Financial (checkboxes)
- Expected Benefits (5 years post-implementation): CCY X.XX M
- *NPV (next 5 years): CCY X.XX M
- Included in Annual Project Plan: Yes / No
- EP Value: X.XX M, Shared IT Infrastructure: X.XX M
  *(EP = Envelope Price: total project cost EXCLUDING shared IT infrastructure)*

**Right column — Timeline Box:**
- Project Duration: XX Months
- Baseline Start: QX YYYY
- Baseline Finish: QX YYYY
- Regulatory Deadline: DD/MM/YYYY

**Right column — Category (select one):**

- Efficiency

## Content Quality Examples

### KPIs — Strong vs Weak

**Strong (has baseline, target, metric, periodicity):**

| Performance Measure | Metric | Baseline | Target | Periodicity |
|--------------------|--------|----------|--------|-------------|
| Customer onboarding time | Minutes per customer | 45 min | 12 min | Monthly |
| Straight-through processing rate | % of transactions | 62% | 95% | Weekly |
| Manual reconciliation effort | FTE hours/month | 320 hrs | 80 hrs | Monthly |

**Weak (will be rejected):**

| Performance Measure | Metric | Baseline | Target | Periodicity |
|--------------------|--------|----------|--------|-------------|
| Improved efficiency | TBD | - | Better | Quarterly |
| Customer satisfaction | Score | N/A | High | Annual |

### Benefits — Strong vs Weak

**Strong:** "USD 2.4M annual cost savings from eliminating 8 FTEs in manual reconciliation
(8 × USD 300K fully loaded cost)"

**Weak:** "Significant cost savings expected from process improvement"

### Risk Mitigations — Strong vs Weak

**Strong:** "Risk: Regulatory approval delay. Mitigation: Pre-engagement with the central bank
started Q1; precedent exists from a similar approval granted to a peer institution last year;
fallback plan to launch Phase 1 without requiring approval."

**Weak:** "Risk: Regulatory delay. Mitigation: Will work with regulators."
