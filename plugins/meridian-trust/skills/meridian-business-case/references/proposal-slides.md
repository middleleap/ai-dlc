## Slide 2: One-Page Pre-Screening Summary (Main Deliverable)

This is the core ISB submission — a dense single-page summary with multiple sections:

### Project Header
- **Project name** and **$** budget indicator
- **Part of budget / quarterly plan:** Yes/No

### Left Column — Project Details
- **Project description:** Short description of what the project is about
- **Key objectives:** e.g., Tap new customer segment, increase efficiency
- **Requestor:** Department and name of the executive
- **Project category:** KSOR / Efficiency / Regulatory & Risk / Strategic

### Center Column — Assessments
- **Compliance & Risk review:** e.g., No critical risk
- **Ops review:** e.g., XX Operations FTEs required for backend processing
- **Other assessments:** e.g., No critical observations

### High-Level Plan Table

| Description | Indicative Timeline | Funding Required |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| MVP to include [features] | 3-4 months | USD X.X Mn |
| Phase 2 [features] | 4-6 months | USD X.X Mn |

### Right Column — Business Assessment
- **Market insights:** e.g., Target segment size and demographics
- **Competitor landscape:** e.g., Limited to no direct competitors
- **Unique value proposition:** e.g., One of its kind offering

### Benefits Overview

**Financial benefits:**
1. Revenue growth — USD X.X Mn within 12 months after go-live
2. Cost reduction — USD X.X Mn in FTE savings
3. Cost avoidance — USD X.X Mn

**Other benefits / Requirements:**
1. TAT reduction — XX% reduction in processing time
2. Potential fines avoidance — USD X.X Mn
3. Regulatory compliance

### Technical Assessment
- **Technology review:** Architecture implications, resource availability
- **T-shirt sizing:** USD X.X Mn estimate
- **Solution selection ("Build" vs "Buy"):** Rationale
- **Key risks & mitigation:** Regulatory approvals, precedent

## Slides 3-4: Key Challenge Questions (Reference Only)

These slides carry the questions the ISB will use to challenge the proposal. Use them to ensure
the proposal addresses all concerns:

### Topic 1: Project Details
- Business rationale? Simpler solution? Why now?

### Topic 2: Business Assessment
- Target segment/market size? Customer preferences?
- Truly unique / differentiator? Competitive landscape?
- Comprehensive risk mitigation plan?

### Topic 3: Technical Assessment
- Existing tech that can address this? Long-term architecture impact?
- Impact on critical systems? Part of an upcoming platform upgrade?

### Topic 4: Other Assessments
- Regulatory considerations? Other observations?

### Topic 5: High-Level Plan
- Break into MVPs? Benefits per MVP?
- Realistic timelines? Acceleration/delay factors?
- Internal (cross-functional) and external (regulatory) factors?

### Topic 6: Benefits Overview
- Key assumptions in benefits calculations? Reasonable?
- How long projected? Long-term financial benefit?
- Non-financial benefits beyond TAT and fines avoidance?

## Slide 5: Solution Selection Framework (Reference)

| Archetype | Build | Buy |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| Description | Custom solution based on bank-specific requirements | Off-the-shelf, customized if necessary |
| Vendor options | Internal development team, custom software vendors | Existing panel vendors, new vendors |
| When to choose | Strong business differentiation, faster TTM | Standard capability, vendor ecosystem |

## Build vs Buy Decision Guide

The ISB expects a justified Build vs Buy recommendation. Use these criteria:

| Factor | Favors Build | Favors Buy |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| Differentiation | Core to the bank's competitive advantage | Standard capability, commodity |
| Time-to-market | In-house team can deliver faster | Vendor solution ready sooner |
| Customization | Highly bank-specific requirements | Configurable off-the-shelf meets 80%+ needs |
| Total cost of ownership | Lower 5-year TCO with internal | Lower 5-year TCO with vendor |
| Maintenance burden | Team has capacity for ongoing support | Prefer vendor-managed updates |
| Vendor landscape | No strong vendors exist | Mature vendor ecosystem |
| Architecture | Must integrate deeply with core systems | Standalone or loosely coupled |

**Always address:** Why was the alternative rejected? What trade-offs were considered?
