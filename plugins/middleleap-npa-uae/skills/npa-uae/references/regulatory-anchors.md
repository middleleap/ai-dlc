# Regulatory anchors — what each field is checked against

References are to the CBUAE Rulebook as published. Article numbering changes; the agent cites the reference and marks it "to be confirmed by Compliance" if it cannot verify the current text.

## The obligation itself

- **Consumer Protection Standards, Article 3 (General Provisions).** Licensed Financial Institutions must apply the Consumer Protection Regulation and Standards to any new product or service, activity, action, change or event (3.1.2.2), and the Board and Senior Management must apply strict product and/or service approval processes before a new or updated financial product or service is released to the market. This is why the NPA exists.
- **Consumer Protection Regulation (Circular 8/2020)** — the principles the Standards implement; conduct, disclosure, suitability, complaints.
- **Risk Management Regulation and Standards (153/2018)** — new products and activities must be assessed within the risk governance framework; risk appetite, the three lines, and Islamic-specific risk provisions.
- **Corporate Governance Regulation and Standards (2019)** — committee structures and Board accountability the sign-off roster reflects.

## Open Finance specifics

When the product uses Open Finance in either role, Section 5.1 must address: the pricing model set by the Central Bank and reviewed with the Nebras Board; the notice period and dual running required for breaking changes; continuing conformance with the Standards version in force and its errata; FAPI 2.0, mTLS and SCA; consent lifecycle and expiry rules; certification paths (OIDF, Functional, CX, Live Proving); and liability allocation between LFI, TPP and Nebras.
