# Meridian obligations — candidate citations for the owners to confirm

**Status: PROPOSAL.** Nothing here has been written into `demo/meridian/open-finance-obligations.json`. Every `article` field in that register still reads "to be verified before citation", on purpose: regulatory text is load-bearing and is never paraphrased from memory into a register (`CLAUDE.md`). This page is the legwork — the live source for each obligation, fetched on **16 September 2026**, quoted verbatim where the source allowed it, with a confidence mark and what is still unsure. The obligation's **owner** confirms and writes the citation; the demo's register changes only after that.

How the sources were reached: the CBUAE Rulebook and the OF Confluence space were fetched directly (plain HTTP client; the Rulebook refuses some automated clients, so per-article pages were used). The community hub (`nebras-open-finance.com`) was fetched directly. The UAE PDPL's official English text (`uaelegislation.gov.ae`) refused automated clients; its article numbers below come from secondary summaries and are marked accordingly.

Two things worth knowing before reading the table:

- The Rulebook carries **both** Open Finance Regulations. Search engines surface the **repealed C 7/2023** article pages first (marked "Status: Repealed"). The in-force text is **C 03/2025, issued 10 July 2025, Status: In-Force**; its article pages carry a `-0` suffix, and the full text is at `entiresection/6127`. Every citation below is from the 2025 text.
- The article *numbering* in the skill's `cbuae-regulations.md` article map matches the 2025 text for every article checked (2, 3, 13, 15, 16, 17, 20, 22, 31, 33, 35, 38).
